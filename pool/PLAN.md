# Nature Pool + Biofilter — Build Plan

Part of the deck project (5.0 × 6.5 m deck, zen sandbox Ø 2.36 m, small block bottom-left).
Date: 2026-08-17

## 1. Layout

| Item | Internal size | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Pool | 2.0 × 3.0 m, 150 cm deep (8 courses = 160 cm wall) | top-left corner of deck |
| Biofilter | 2.0 × 2.0 m nominal, 100 cm deep (5 courses) | directly below pool, shares one 2 m wall. Sandbox circle (centre ~2.2 m from left edge, 4.6 m from back edge) cuts into the filter's right side; the filter wall follows the circle as a cast concrete arc (r ≈ 1.3 m, ~2.4 m long, 15 cm). Net planted area ≈ 1.6 m² (26 % of pool surface; pumped up-flow bog filter is normally sized 10–30 %). Can be extended 60 cm south for 2.2 m² |
| Tops | flush with each other, at deck level | filter floor sits 50 cm above pool floor |

Combined footprint (outer): ~2.3 × 5.45 m (15 cm walls; 19 cm walls on pool → ~2.4 × 5.6 m).

## 2. Structure

- Cast reinforced concrete slab, 15 cm, C25/30, on 15 cm compacted stabilgrus.
- Walls: RBR Fundamentblok 20 × 50 cm (H × L), laid in mortar, every cell filled with concrete.
  - Pool walls: **19 cm** width (1.5 m water + backfill pressure).
  - Filter walls: 15 cm width is fine.
- Rebar: Ø10 vertical every 50 cm cast into slab as starters, Ø8 horizontal in every course, continuous through corners.
- Interior: skim/render to flush, mortar coves (hulkehl, ~5 cm radius) at all floor/wall joints.
- Waterproofing: **Mapei Mapelastic** (grey, 2-comp), 2 coats, min 2 mm, mesh/Mapeband at joints and pipe penetrations. Left bare grey = natural look, biofilm covers within a season.
  - Alternative: Vandex Cemelast / BB 75 (Vandex A/S, DK, tel 8747 7477).
  - Optional face: 8–10 mm vandtæt puds (Vandex Uni 1 or cement render + Sika-1), sponge-float, jernoxid pigment.

## 3. Water flow — up-flow bog filter, gravity return

```
POOL --skimmer/pump--> 50 mm pipe --> FILTER bottom manifold
  ^                                    (perforated pipes under gravel)
  |                                         | water rises through gravel + roots
  +---- weir notch in shared wall <---------+ (filter level ~5-8 cm above pool level)
```

- Pump lifts water into filter bottom; water rises through gravel; overflows back to pool by gravity.
- Weir: 30–40 cm wide, ~5 cm deep notch in top course of shared wall. Flat stone in notch = small cascade.
- No low opening between pool and filter — filter must drain independently.
- Bottom manifold has its own 50 mm drain through outer wall to a ball valve → backflush to garden 1–2×/year.
- Pump: Oase AquaMax Eco Premium 6000 (6 m³/h, ~45 W). Pool ~8.4 m³ water → turnover ~1.5 h.
- Intake: Oase AquaSkim skimmer so surface film goes to filter.

## 5. Build order

1. Dig, stabilgrus, compact.
2. Slab with mesh + Ø10 starters every 50 cm along wall lines. Cure ≥ 7 days.
3. Blocks in mortar, rebar each course. Set pipe sleeves in wall (pump line, filter drain). Form weir notch.
4. Fill cells with concrete, rod/vibrate. Cure 28 days.
5. Skim + coves. Mapelastic 2 coats, mesh at joints. Cure 7 days.
6. Fill, soak 1 week, drain (cement pH), refill.
7. Manifold, gravel, plants, pump. Run.
