# Rendering prompt — deck + nature pool in the garden

Attach 3 images in this order:
1. **Garden photo** (base — camera, perspective, everything that stays)

## Prompt

Photorealistic architectural visualisation. Use IMAGE 1 (the garden photo) as the base: keep its exact camera position, lens, perspective, sky, the tall hedge at the back and left, the pink hibiscus on the left, the neighbour's house roof, the brick house wall and downpipe on the right, and the existing wooden privacy screen with the small deck in the lower right. Remove the loose timber beams on the ground, the black bucket, the lawn mower, toys and the potted grass in the foreground.

Build the new terrace on the bare patch and lawn exactly according to the plan in IMAGE 2 (top-down). Orientation: the top edge of the plan is the far end against the back hedge; the left edge of the plan runs along the left hedge; the right edge joins the existing small deck by the privacy screen; the bottom edge faces the camera. Keep the plan proportions: overall 5.0 m wide × 6.5 m deep, with the chamfered (45°) corner at the near-right.

Elements, exactly as in the plan:
- Deck: thermo-treated pine boards 26×140 mm, warm honey-brown, smooth, 5 mm gaps, boards running left-right, deck top 30 cm above the lawn with a fascia board on the visible edges. It wraps around all elements below.
- Nature pool 2×3 m at the far-left corner: still, clear dark-green water, flush with the deck, walls of grey mineral render, no tiles, no blue liner. Deck boards run to the water's edge.
- Bio-filter 2×2 m directly in front of the pool (between pool and sandbox): a planted gravel bed at deck level, dense yellow iris, reed, rushes and water mint, with a small flat-stone spillway where clear water trickles from the filter over the shared wall into the pool.
- Zen sandbox: a perfect circle Ø 2.36 m cut into the deck in front of the filter, edged with a thin rusted corten-steel ring, filled with fine white raked sand in concentric ripples around 2–3 dark stones.
- Tech/storage box: a low rectangular box 0.5×1.5 m, 50 cm high, clad in the same thermo pine, at the near-left corner of the deck.
- Right side of the deck stays open as a lounge area (optional: a compact outdoor sofa set with sand-coloured cushions and a low wood table).

Style, light and mood copied from IMAGE 3: late golden hour / blue-hour dusk, orange-pink sky, warm recessed LED deck lights along the edges, soft warm uplights in the hedge and among the filter plants, warm string lights, long soft shadows, calm and premium Scandinavian garden atmosphere. Do NOT copy the pergola or the pool position from IMAGE 3 unless it matches the plan — the plan in IMAGE 2 wins over IMAGE 3 for geometry.

Ultra-detailed, physically correct reflections in the water, realistic wood grain, 16:9, high resolution, no text, no people, no watermark.

## Variants
- Daytime: replace the light paragraph with "midday summer sun as in IMAGE 1, natural daylight, crisp shadows, no artificial lights".
- With pergola: add "a slim black steel pergola with a light fabric canopy over the lounge area at the far-right, string lights, as in IMAGE 3".
- No furniture: delete the lounge sentence.

## Tips
- If the model ignores geometry, say: "Treat IMAGE 2 as a strict floor plan; every element must appear in the position and proportion shown."
- If it moves the pool, add: "The pool is in the FAR-LEFT corner, its long side along the left hedge."
- Iterate one fix per pass; re-attach all three images every time.
