# Deck + Zen Sandbox — Build Plan

Companion to `pool/PLAN.md`. Date: 2026-08-17

## 1. Footprint

- 5.0 m wide × 6.5 m deep, bottom-right corner chamfered 1.5 × 1.5 m (2.12 m diagonal).
- Right edge 5.0 m, then 1.5 m in, 2.12 m diagonal, 2.0 m bottom edge (incl. small block ~0.5 × 1.5 m at bottom-left).
- Openings: pool 2 × 3 m (top-left), biofilter 2 × 2 m (below pool), sandbox Ø 2.36 m centred ~2.2 m from left edge / 4.6 m from back edge — it overlaps the filter's right side; filter wall is cast as an arc around the ring.
- Deck top **30 cm above ground**. Small block bottom-left = **tech/storage box** 0.5 × 1.5 m, 50 cm high, standing ON the deck along the left edge at the front (below the filter). Filter's SW corner gets a small notch where the box overlaps (~30 × 40 cm).

## 2. Materials

### Boards — Thermo Fyr 26 × 140 mm glat/glat, 49,95 kr/m
- Pitch 140 + 5 mm gap = 145 mm → 6.9 m board per m².
- 20.5 m² × 6.9 = 141 m, +15 % waste (diagonal, circle, coping) → **~165 m ≈ 32 × 5100 mm** (254,75 kr/stk).
  Mix lengths to suit: rows beside pool/filter zone ~3.3 m long, rows in bottom 1.5 m full 5.1 m.
- Fasteners: **A4 rustfri** terrasseskruer 4.5 × 60 (thermo wood is acidic; galv. rusts/stains). ~600–700 stk.

### Substructure — trykimprægneret
- Bjælker 45 × 195 on ground screws, c/c ~2.0 m, spanning ≤ 2.0 m between screws: ~26 m.
- Strøer 45 × 95 c/c **500 mm max** (26 mm thermo pine), on top of bjælker, doubled under board end joints: ~55 m.
- Strøbånd / asfaltpap on every strø top.
- Beslag: vinkelbeslag strø→bjælke, U-beslag/plade on screw head.

### Ground screws (jordskruer) — no concrete
- ~68 × 865 mm (or 76 × 1000 mm) with U-bracket or flange, ~250 kr/stk.
- Grid c/c ~2.0 × 2.0 m → **~16 stk**. Extra 2–3 at chamfer and small block.
- Drive with rental tool (~500 kr/day) or T-bar by hand in sandy soil.
- Level: adjust bracket, or trim bjælke — deck top must meet pool wall top.

**Backfill warning:** ground screws in loose backfill around the pool hole will sink. Either
(a) backfill with stabilgrus in 20 cm compacted layers, or
(b) keep screws outside the dug zone and let strøer near the pool bear on the pool wall top via a lath + rubber pad, not fixed — deck and pool must be able to move independently.

### Sandbox — Corten ring Ø 236 cm, 15 cm high, 3 mm, 1 270,75 kr
- Sections bolted, comes with samlebeslag.
- Deck is ~20–30 cm above ground on screws → ring must be raised: base of 10–15 cm compacted stabilgrus + geotextile so ring top ≈ deck top (or 1–2 cm proud). Boards cut to circle with ~10 mm gap.
- Sand: 4.4 m² × 10 cm = 0.44 m³ ≈ 0.7 t. Zen look: kvartssand 0.4–0.8 mm, ~28 × 25 kg. Cheap: sandkassesand.
- Small drainage: grus base drains itself; no bottom needed. Weed cloth under sand.

## 5. Build order (deck after pool walls + backfill)

1. Pool/filter done to filled walls and backfilled + compacted.
2. Set out screw grid, drive screws, level heads to pool wall top minus (26 + 45 + 195 mm).
3. Bjælker on screws, strøer c/c 500 across, strøbånd.
4. Sandbox: dig/compact grus base, assemble corten ring, set level with deck top.
5. Boards from pool edge outward, 5 mm gaps, A4 screws, 2 per strø. Cut circle around ring, diagonal at chamfer.
6. Fill sand.

## 6. Height stack (30 cm)

Screw head ~35 mm above ground + bjælke 45×195 + strø 45×95 + board 26 = ~300 mm. Use 45×145 bjælke if screws end up higher.
